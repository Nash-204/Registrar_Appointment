PUT YOUR IMAGES IN THIS FOLDER
==============================

  logo.png   Your office / municipal seal.
             Square, at least 256 x 256 px, transparent background works best.

  hero.jpg   The big photo behind the home page headline (for example, the Municipal Hall).
             Wide, at least 1920 x 800 px. The left side is darkened so the white text stays readable,
             so keep the main subject toward the right.

Use exactly those file names and the site picks them up automatically.
After replacing a file, refresh the browser with Ctrl + F5.

Want different names or formats (.webp, .svg, .jpeg)? Change the paths at the top of
client/src/siteConfig.js.

Until the files exist, the site shows a simple icon instead of the logo and a navy background
instead of the photo, so nothing looks broken.
