CIVIL REGISTRY REQUIREMENTS: LEGITIMATION

1. COURT DECREE REGISTRATION (LEGITIMATION / JUDICIAL ORDERS)
   - Certified photocopy of the Court Decision
   - Certified true copy of Certificate of Finality

2. STANDARD LEGITIMATION BY SUBSEQUENT MARRIAGE OF PARENTS
   - Certificate of Live Birth (PSA copy)
   - Certificate of Marriage of parents (PSA copy)
   - Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) of both parents prior to marriage (PSA copy)
   - Joint Affidavit of Legitimation executed by both parents
