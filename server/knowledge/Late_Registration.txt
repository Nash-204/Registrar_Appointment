CIVIL REGISTRY REQUIREMENTS: LATE REGISTRATION

1. LATE REGISTRATION OF BIRTH
   - Certificate of Live Birth / Municipal Form 102
   - Negative Certification of Birth (issued by PSA)
   - Affidavit of Two Disinterested Persons
   - Documentary evidence (Baptismal / Islam certificate, baby's record, school record, voter certification, etc.)
   - Valid identification card
   - Marriage Certificate (if married)
   - Barangay Certification (issued by the Punong Barangay as proof of residency)
   - National ID (if not yet registered with PhilSys, registrant must register before processing)
   - Any two (2) documentary evidence showing identity of parents (e.g., Certificate of Live Birth, government-issued ID, marriage certificate of parents, or death certificate of deceased parents)
   - Unedited front-facing photo of registrant (2x2 size, white background, taken within 3 months)

   * Additional Requirements for Marital Child with Foreign Parent:
     - Certificate of Marriage of parents
     - Birth certificate of parent/s
     - Valid Passport OR BI Clearance Certificate OR ACR I-Card of the foreign parent

   * Additional Requirements for Non-Marital Child with Foreign Parent:
     - Affidavit of Admission of Paternity (AAP) and/or Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF) under R.A. 9255, OR
     - Affidavit of Acknowledgment (for non-marital child born before August 03, 1988)

2. LATE REGISTRATION OF DEATH
   - Certificate of Death / Municipal Form 103
   - Negative Certification of Death (issued by PSA)
   - Affidavit of Two Disinterested Persons
   - Proof of Death (e.g., picture of epitaph)

3. LATE REGISTRATION OF MARRIAGE
   - Marriage Certificate / Municipal Form 97
   - Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR issued by PSA)
   - Affidavit of Two Disinterested Persons
   - Affidavit of Late Registration by registrant
