CIVIL REGISTRY REQUIREMENTS: CORRECTION OF BIRTH / DEATH / MARRIAGE

1. CORRECTION OF CLERICAL ERRORS (R.A. 9048)
   - PSA copy of the civil registry document sought to be corrected (Certificate of Live Birth / Certificate of Marriage / Certificate of Death)
   - Supporting Documents (at least 2 or as applicable):
     * Baptismal / Dedication certificate
     * School records (Diploma, Transcript of Records [TOR], Form 137, etc.)
     * Medical records
     * Marriage certificate
     * Birth certificate of parents / children (if applicable)
     * Death certificate of parents (if applicable)
     * Valid identification cards (e.g. Passport, GSIS/SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Driver's License, etc.)
     * Voter Certification
   - Fees:
     * Filing fee: P1,000 (Exemption: Indigent with certificate from DSWD)
     * Miscellaneous fee: P400
     * Service fee for migrant petition: P500

2. CHANGE OF FIRST NAME (R.A. 9048)
   - PSA copy of the Certificate of Live Birth of the person whose first name is sought to be changed
   - Supporting Documents:
     * Baptismal certificate
     * School records (Diploma, TOR)
     * Medical records
     * Valid Identification cards (Driver's license, Passport, SSS/GSIS ID, PRC ID, etc.)
     * Voter certification
     * Marriage certificate
     * Birth certificates of children
   - Clearances required:
     * Employer clearance (if employed, indicating no pending administrative case) OR Affidavit of Unemployment (if not employed)
     * PNP Clearance
     * NBI Clearance
   - Affidavit of Publication with newspaper clipping from publisher (publication for 2 consecutive weeks)
   - Fees:
     * Filing fee: P3,000 (Exemption: Indigent with certificate from DSWD)
     * Miscellaneous fee: P400
     * Service fee for Migrant Petition: P1,000

3. CORRECTION OF SEX / DAY & MONTH OF BIRTH IN THE COLB (R.A. 10172)
   - PSA copy of the Certificate of Live Birth sought to be corrected
   - LCRO copy of the Birth Certificate
   - Earliest school record / document (Elementary Form 137) OR Affidavit of No School Record (if never entered school)
   - Medical Record OR Affidavit of No Medical Record (if never had medical check-up)
   - Baptismal / Dedication certificate OR Affidavit of No Baptismal Certificate (if never baptized)
   - Clearances:
     * Employer clearance (indicating no pending administrative case) OR Affidavit of Unemployment
     * NBI Clearance
     * PNP Clearance
   - Affidavit of Publication with newspaper clipping from publisher (publication for 2 consecutive weeks)
   - Other documents which the CCR may require
   - Fees:
     * Filing fee: P3,000 (Exemption: Indigent with certificate from DSWD)
     * Miscellaneous fee: P400
     * Service fee for Migrant Petition: P1,000
