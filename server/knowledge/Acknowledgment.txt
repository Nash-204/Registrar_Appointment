CIVIL REGISTRY REQUIREMENTS: ACKNOWLEDGMENT

1. FOR NON-MARITAL CHILD BORN ON OR AFTER AUGUST 3, 1988:
   - Affidavit of Admission of Paternity (AAP) under R.A. 9255
   - Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF) under R.A. 9255

2. FOR NON-MARITAL CHILD BORN BEFORE AUGUST 3, 1988:
   - Affidavit of Acknowledgment

3. FOR NON-MARITAL CHILD WITH FOREIGN PARENT:
   - Affidavit of Admission of Paternity (AAP) and/or Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF) under R.A. 9255, OR
   - Affidavit of Acknowledgment (if born before August 3, 1988)
   - Valid Passport, BI Clearance Certificate, or ACR I-Card of the foreign parent
