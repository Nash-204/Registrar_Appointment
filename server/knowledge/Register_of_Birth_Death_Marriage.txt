CIVIL REGISTRY REQUIREMENTS: REGISTER OF BIRTH / DEATH / MARRIAGE

1. MARRIAGE LICENSE / MARRIAGE REGISTRATION
   - Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) - PSA
   - Birth Certificate - PSA
   - Community Tax Certificate (CTC / Cedula)
   - Certificate of Compliance / Certificate of Marriage Counseling from POPCOM office
   - Certificate of Tree Planting (2 seedlings) from the Barangay Chairman
   - 2 pictures of the actual tree planting
   - Identification / Advice Forms to be signed by parents
   - Certificate of Legal Capacity to Marry from Embassy Concerned (If one party is a foreigner)

2. MARRIAGE UNDER ARTICLE 34 (EXEMPTION FROM MARRIAGE LICENSE)
   - Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) - PSA
   - Birth Certificate - PSA
   - Birth Certificate of the child / children
   - Affidavit of Cohabitation
   - Affidavit of Two (2) Disinterested Persons (Witnesses)
